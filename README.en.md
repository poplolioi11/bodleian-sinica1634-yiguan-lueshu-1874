# 醫館略述三書(1874;Bodleian Sinica 1634) / Yi guan lue shu san shu [Brief account of a medical institution: Three writings]: Transcription dataset

Yi guan lue shu san shu [Brief account of a medical institution: Three writings] is a Chinese hospital publication catalogued as 1874, with [Foochow] supplied as its place of publication and 柯為樑 (Dauphin William Osgood) credited with revision. Its charitable and missionary statements, hospital narratives, illustrations, donor lists and financial tables support research on medical charity, fundraising and institutional publishing. Based on 24 images of Bodleian Sinica 1634, the dataset provides TXT, JSON and TEI XML for 42 designated physical faces, with uncertainties and source mappings. The provisional AI transcription includes selected source-image corrections; full human double collation and expert review have not been performed.
